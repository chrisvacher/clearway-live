# DiscoverTheme

One Discover theme: its label, what it rests on (27 of 87, 4 of 5 interviews), example quotes with no names, its source, the AI drafted marker, the thin flag, and Accept, Rename, Merge or Reject. ThemeList orders them by count and animates every change.

**Screens:** SET-04, SET-05, SET-06. **Sets:** Setup. Design pass 6 (milestone 6).

**Consumer provides:** The theme (label, count, total, kind survey or interviews, source, extra sources, quotes), its state (draft, accepted), and any rename or merge history.

**Anatomy:** Label at app-body 700 with renamed-from or merged-from notes, the count large with of 87 or of 5 interviews, a thin bar for survey themes, source chips, AI drafted until accepted, the thin flag, up to two quotes with a left rule, and the actions. Accepted adds a Success edge, the chip and Undo.

| State | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | AI drafted marker, all actions |
| Accepted | Success edge, Accepted, Undo |
| Renamed | The label crossfades, Renamed from |
| Merged | Glides into its survivor, counts add, Merged from 2 |
| Rejected | Leaves, list closes up, Restore at the foot |
| Thin | Thin flag with the reason |
| Growing | Count rolls, bar grows, themes trade places |
| AI off | No drafts; answers listed to group by hand |

**Don't:** Put a name on a quote, or let a draft reach What We Heard or the signal.
