Milestone 6 screens

Every screen in the design pass 6 brief, labelled with its screen IDs and the component names they'll have in code: the congregational survey read from Team Hub (setup and the room), the Four Helpful Lists, interviews and input conversations, What We Heard from accepted themes, and the alignment signal on the facilitator's private view and own remote. Built on the Clearway Live design system and the approved pass 2 to 5 work, with Riverside Church's seed data (base year 2026: 87 survey responses, 6 of them under 30, 5 interviews and 7 elders). Each board moves between its states without redrawing: pick a state, switch the TV between Navy and Off-White, and use the buttons to watch input arrive. The decisions board is first and the six new movements are at the end.
