Milestone 4 screens

Every screen in the design pass 4 brief, labelled with its screen IDs and the component names they'll have in code, built on the Clearway Live design system and the pass 2 and 3 live room with Riverside Church's seed data (base year 2026). Each interaction board keeps one live TV that moves between its states without redrawing: pick a state, switch between the Navy and Off-White themes, and use the buttons to watch input arrive. The five new movements are at the end with their tokens and reduced-motion equivalents.
