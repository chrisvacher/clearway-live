# ScribeFeed

The scribe's cards arriving in the control surface's private panel while a session is live, with Promote to TV on a quote.

**Screens:** FAC-01. **Sets:** Control. Design pass 5 (milestone 5).

**Consumer provides:** this session's cards newest first, the promoted quote, sync state.

**Anatomy:** Inside the "Only you see this" panel: up to four compact ScribeCards, newest first, then "+ N more cards on the scribe console". A quote carries Promote to TV, or On the TV and Take off the TV. No speaker names.

| State | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Typical | Cards land at the top and the rest make room |
| Quote on the TV | The card shows On the TV and Take off the TV |
| Empty | Says where cards will appear |
| Crowded | Four newest and a count |
| Offline | Queued cards arrive in capture order, a stagger apart |
