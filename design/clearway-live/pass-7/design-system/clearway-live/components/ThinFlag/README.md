# ThinFlag

The thin data marker with its reason, readable without color: a dashed outline, the circle-dashed icon and the words Thin data. A theme or segment is thin under 10 responses, under 10 percent of respondents, or on one interview.

**Screens:** Every Discover screen. **Sets:** Setup, control, TV, phone. Design pass 6 (milestone 6).

**Consumer provides:** The reason (6 of 87 respondents, one interview) and the surface.

**Anatomy:** App: a 24px dashed marker in cwl-app-text-muted with Thin data and the reason in muted text. TV and phone: the cwl-marker shape with a dashed border in cwl-border-strong. The rule is code, never AI.

| State | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| App | Dashed marker with reason |
| TV | Marker at tv-floor, dashed |
| Phone | Marker at phone-micro, dashed |

**Don't:** Use color alone, or hide a thin result.
