## What milestone 4 builds

- **Lens seeding and the lens wall (INT-12).** A self-paced seeding session opens during the pre-call and stays open until Day 1. When each lens opens, its seeded sentences arrive first, then new present-tense sentences land as they're sent, and similar sentences drift into clusters under growing labels.
- **Cluster and rename (INT-13).** AI proposes 6 to 10 candidate priorities from all Five Lenses, each with its source sentences, on the control surface first. Phones rename, merge or suggest, the facilitator accepts each with one tap, then 100 points (INT-07) with a cut line set between four and six, and the facilitator marks the provisional Vision Outcomes.
- **Outcome bullets.** One Vision Outcome at a time: "In 2029, what's true?" Bullets fill in under the heading. An outcome with no bullets stays outlined in Sunrise until it has one (code). Bullets are the outcome's indicators.
- **Outcome confirm.** Phones confirm, rename or challenge each provisional outcome, with an optional reason. The TV shows the counts, and a challenged outcome lifts on the control surface and gets a soft highlight on the TV until decided. The facilitator records the room's consensus decision and reasons (Mark a decision). Counts never decide. The app warns, without blocking, outside four to six. Each outcome gets a one-line description drafted by the facilitator.
- **The pre-mortem.** The 2x2 grid (INT-08) with its Pre-mortem preset: "It's 2029 and this plan failed. Why?" AI merges duplicate reasons, with undo.
- **The CORE goal builder (INT-15).** One outcome at a time, its 3-year Vision bullets left and its 1-year goals right. A goal has a title, a result, one owner, an end date (defaulting to a season end) and the risk it answers. Each CORE letter shows met or missing, checked in code.
- **Handing over the remote (D2-11) and the commander's intent card (INT-16).** The team-led block from commander's intent through affirming the cards, with field locks, typed lines replacing on the TV, and first steps flagged without an owner or date. Affirm together reuses sign together (INT-10).
- **The draft Clearway Map (D2-15).** Assembled from the engagement at the close of Day 2. Empty fields show as empty, never invented. This Year's Priorities is empty: left for the church's Annual Planning.
- **Scripture and prayer screens** in their Wayfinding places (PRE-06 on 1 Corinthians 3:5-8, New Living Translation, at `tv-body`, D1-13, D2-01), on step 1's content screens.
- **Lens identity.** The Five Lenses share one lens color (`cwl-lens`, Royal, and `cwl-lens-small` below 20px) and are told apart by name and the five drawn icons in `pass-4/design-system/clearway-live/assets/Icons/`, the same on every surface. Lens names come from the engagement (Customer replaces Congregation for an organization that isn't a church).
- **The templates (SET-08).** The Wayfinding and day-and-a-half templates ship with the app, built on step 6b's templates.

Every prompt fills its years from the engagement's base year (10-year Future 2036, 3-year Vision 2029, 1-year Outlook 2027 for a 2026 base year) and its lens names from the engagement. Horizon terms are written exactly: 10-year Future, 3-year Vision, 1-year Outlook.

## The engine contract

Milestone 3's engine (`docs/interaction-engine.md`, and the contract in `docs/milestone-3-brief.md`) is unchanged. Every Wayfinding kind registers itself in `private.interaction_kinds` and supplies its own `parse_`, `results_`, `options_` and `items_` functions, plus one line each in `tv-views.tsx`, `phone-inputs.tsx` and `option-editors.tsx`. The engine still supplies one answer per person with replacement, private kinds, bursts and phones down, Spotlight and Hide, late answers in a segment, self-paced windows, test answers, CSV export and the recap. Every number on screen is computed once on the server.

**New kinds** (reserve these names):

| Kind | Interaction | Reuses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lens` | Lens seeding (self-paced) and the lens wall (INT-12) | The wall's answers, word filter and step 5's group path (`group-wall`), with the lens and "Seeded from" |
| `candidates` | Cluster and rename (INT-13) | Add an option's suggestion flow (`ranking_suggest`, one-tap accept) for renames, merges and suggestions |
| `bullets` | Outcome bullets | The wall's text answers, filed under a Vision Outcome |
| `confirm` | Outcome confirm | One answer per person per outcome, Mark a decision, and INT-13's accepted renames |
| `goals` | CORE goal builder (INT-15) | Structured answers, the attendee list for owners |
| `intent` | Commander's intent card (INT-16) | Structured answers, with field locks and typed edits |

**Kinds reused as they are:** `points` across the accepted candidates (cut line set between four and six), `grid` with its Pre-mortem preset (and `grid_merges` for duplicate reasons), `sign` for Affirm together, `ranking` with Add an option for the metrics, `word_cloud`, `wall`, `qa` and `content` (Scripture, prayer, headline with depth, holding). The draft Clearway Map is a screen with no input.

**Wayfinding records.** The engagement holds the outputs the PRD's data model lists for this milestone: lens sentences (seeded or live), Vision Outcomes (title, description, provisional or confirmed, indicators, what we'll stop, order), risks, goals, intent cards and first steps, key metrics, celebrations, decisions and the Clearway Map draft. Kinds write to them through their own functions. Row-level security on every table, with tests by role.

**Rules every Wayfinding kind keeps**

- Every input reaches the TV in under 1 second (95 in 100) and animates in. Nothing redraws, refreshes or waits for a reveal.
- Seeded sentences never leave the database before their lens opens: not to the TV, phones, the control surface, the remote, exports or the recap. The facilitator gets only the count.
- Checks that follow a rule are code, not AI: the empty-outcome flag, the CORE letters, first steps missing an owner or date, the 100-point total, four to six confirmed outcomes.
- Owners of goals and first steps come from the attendee list or are added by name and email, one person each.
- Names never show on the TV. A team facilitator's remote never shows the alignment signal, presenter notes, Discover data or other blocks.

## Work order

One branch and one pull request per step. Migrations are approved and applied one at a time, after each pull request merges, then renamed to the version the database recorded.

1. **Brief, Wayfinding records and shared pieces.** This brief; the Wayfinding records above and their security; "Seeded from" on the run of show; Mark a decision on the control surface and remote; lens identity (LensIcon, the lens heading with the drawn icons, lens names from the engagement); the Scripture and prayer screens in their Wayfinding places (PRE-06, D1-13, D2-01).
2. **Lens seeding and the lens wall** (INT-12, PRE-10, PRE-11, D1-05, D1-06). Seeds hidden until each lens opens, then arriving first. Live clustering by AI with rename, undo, pin and off.
3. **Cluster and rename to provisional outcomes** (INT-13, D1-07 to D1-10). AI candidates on the control surface first, renames, merges and suggestions, 100 points with the cut line, Mark provisional.
4. **Outcome bullets and outcome confirm** (D1-11, D1-12, D2-05, D2-06). The empty-outcome flag, the confirm sweep, challenged outcomes, the consensus decision, the four-to-six warning, the one-line description.
5. **Pre-mortem and the CORE goal builder** (D2-07 to D2-10, INT-15). AI merges of duplicate reasons, goals with their CORE letters, owners, season-end dates and risk links.
6. **Handover, commander's intent and affirming** (D2-11 to D2-14, INT-16). The remote changing hands, pair assignment, field locks, lines replacing as people type, first-step flags, Affirm together, the remote returning after the block, no scribe console in the block.
7. **The draft Map and the templates** (D2-15, SET-08, and wiring PRE-06 to PRE-09, D1-04, D2-02 into the template). The Wayfinding and day-and-a-half templates with their prompts, timings, notes, segments and team-led block.
8. **Acceptance kit.** Every Wayfinding interaction through the live-screen rules with 50 simulated phones, the failure kit extended, and the milestone 4 practice-room script with the Riverside engagement run from the pre-call to the draft Map.

## In parallel

Step 1 goes first and alone. After it merges, steps 2, 4, 5 and 6 run as parallel agents in separate worktrees, each owning its kinds, components, functions and migration. Steps 4 to 6 work from Vision Outcomes in step 1's records (seeded for Riverside in their tests), so they don't wait for step 3. Their pull requests merge one at a time, each rebased on the last; the registry lines are the only shared edits. Step 6 alone redefines the room's remote controls for the handover. Step 3 follows step 2, since its candidates are drawn from the lens sentences and their clusters. Step 7 follows steps 2 to 6, since the Map reads every record and the templates use every kind. Step 8 is last.

## AI

As milestone 3 step 5 built it (`docs/ai-live.md`): AI is never in the path from phone to TV. An answer is stored and broadcast first, and AI results arrive later as their own room events that rearrange what's already on screen. The worker's claim, apply and fail with one lease per session, the merge limits in code, the AI switches per engagement and session, the run log (model, prompt version, inputs, output, tokens, cost) and FAIL-09 all carry over.

| Job | Step | Reaches the room how | Prompt |

| Cluster lens sentences | 2 | Directly, labels marked AI, with rename, undo, pin and off | A lens version of `group-wall`, keeping the present tense |
| Propose 6 to 10 candidate priorities from all Five Lenses, each with its sources | 3 | Control surface first. The TV only when the facilitator sends them | New, on request |
| Merge duplicate pre-mortem reasons | 5 | Directly, with undo | New |

- The fast live model (`CLEARWAY_AI_MODEL_LIVE`). Inputs carry text and counts under short refs, never attendee identity fields.
- Prompts live in `lib/ai/prompts.ts` with a version each, use Clearway's terms exactly (Vision Outcomes, the horizon terms, the engagement's lens names), and are tested against fixtures from the fictional Riverside engagement in `tests/fixtures/ai/`: folds what should fold, never invents content, keeps present tense, never names people. ## Acceptance criteria (PRD, milestone 4)

- Seeded sentences stay hidden from everyone, the facilitator included, until the lens opens. The facilitator sees only how many have been written.
- Clustering never delays a sentence reaching the TV. The facilitator can undo, pin or turn it off. AI candidates reach the TV only when the facilitator sends them.
- Confirming an outcome records the room's consensus decision and its reasons, and is never decided by a count. The app warns when fewer than four or more than six are confirmed.
- A goal shows each CORE letter as met or missing, checked in code, and stays flagged until all four are met. Owners come from the attendee list or are added by name and email, one person each. End dates default to a season end.
- In the two-day template, each goal links to at least one pre-mortem risk. In the day-and-a-half template, risk links are added after the follow-up session. Each Vision Outcome records what the church will stop.
- Pairs are assigned from the remote or picked on phones. Two phones can edit the same intent card, with one person per field at a time, and edits replace the old line on the TV as they're typed.
- A first step without an owner or a date is flagged on the card.
- The team facilitator's remote hides the alignment signal, presenter notes and other sessions. The facilitator's remote shows who has it, can take it back without the TV changing, and gets it back automatically after the block.
- There is no scribe console in the team-led block.
- The draft Clearway Map fills from the engagement and shows empty fields as empty. This Year's Priorities is empty, left for Annual Planning.
- Every prompt shows the right years for the base year, and the right lens names.
- Every Wayfinding interaction passes the live-screen rules with 50 simulated phones.
