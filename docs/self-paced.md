## Self-paced sessions

- **Times decide.** A session set to Self-paced in the run of show has open and close times (already stored since milestone 2; the run of show requires both, open before close). Its stored state stays Draft or Ready. `private.session_state(session, at)` works out the rest whenever the session is read or answered: Ready before it opens, Open between its times, Closed after. There is no scheduled job and no new service, so a self-paced session opens and closes on time even if nothing else happens in the engagement, and moving its close time later opens it again.
- **Outside the live room.** Go live refuses a self-paced session and Next and Back skip it (milestone 2's rule, unchanged). Close a part leaves it alone: its own times close it. Its answers never reach the TV: the room event carries no text for it.
- **On phones.** The phone reads `self_paced` with its own view and works each state out from the server's clock between reads, so the session opens and closes on screen on time, and the phone reads its view again at each boundary (a not yet open session carries its title and times only; the prompt, depth and options arrive when it opens). The waiting screen, a looked-back step and a self-paced step list the others under "On your phone", each with "Open until Tue, Nov 10, 9:00 p.m." or "Opens ..." in the church's time zone, never under a live step taking answers. A self-paced step shows "Self-paced", its window, the person's saved answers with Change and Withdraw while open, and "This step is closed. Your answers are kept." after. "Back to now" returns to the room.
- **From the invite email.** Each invite lists every self-paced session that is ready and not yet closed under "Before we meet", with its window and the person's own link: the room link with their email filled in and `step=<session>`. After signing in, the phone opens that session. No email is sent by the tests: the browser kit reads the invite from a local mailbox standing in for Resend.
- **On the control surface.** A Self-paced list under the run of show shows each session's state (Opens later, Open, Closed, Draft), its window and how many people answered, with its CSV.
- Not covered: Q&A votes and Add an option belong to steps 3 and 4 (`qa_vote`, `ranking_suggest`) and still take presses only on live steps; a self-paced Q&A takes questions but not votes. "Seeded from" is milestone 4.

## Test answers

**How a submission is marked a test answer.** A facilitator starts a rehearsal on the engagement (Start rehearsal on the control surface). While it runs, every submission, Q&A vote and suggested option stored for the engagement is marked `test`, by a before insert trigger (`private.mark_test`), so every path that stores one is covered: answers, Questions any time, votes and suggestions. A rehearsal is not a property of a person or a device: anyone rehearsing on a real phone is marked the same way, and the facilitator does not have to set up test attendees. The control surface shows Rehearsing in its header and the count of test answers; phones show "Rehearsal. Your answers are test answers and will be cleared." so nobody mistakes it for the real session. End rehearsal stops the marking; answers already marked stay test answers.

**Clear test answers** (the Rehearsal panel, a second tap within 3 seconds):

- Refused while a session is live (on the TV and phones): the button is held with "Choose Nothing live first", and the database refuses it too (`live`).
- Removes every test answer in the engagement (soft deleted, as the practice reset does), with their latency rows, Q&A question records, folds and votes, and every test vote and suggested option.
- A real answer that a test answer replaced (one answer per person) stands again.
- Every live step the rehearsal reached that now holds no answers goes back to Ready and unreached, so the real session runs it from the start. A step reached before the rehearsal, or holding real answers, keeps its state.
- Ends the rehearsal, raises the room version once so every surface recounts, and clears a spotlit test question.
- Is logged in `private.rehearsal_log` (also every start and end) with who cleared and how many answers, votes, suggestions and steps; the control surface shows "Last cleared Nov 3, 9:40 a.m. by Chris: 12 test answers." Presses carry an id: a repeated press does nothing.

## Export

`GET /api/control/:id/export?session=<id>` (facilitators only; `private.session_export`) downloads one session's input as CSV, oldest first, every standing answer (withdrawn and replaced answers left out), in the church's time zone. Privacy is decided in the database: names travel only where the session is named (sign together, or a session set to named) or private (the form, whose answers go only to the facilitators, with names); an anonymous session exports without a Name column. Signatures export as names, never strokes. Phones, TVs and facilitators not on the engagement are refused. Columns follow the kind: the answer for text kinds; Q&A's question, votes, answered and the question it folded into; one column per option for choice (1 for a pick), ranking (the place) and 100 points; the value for scale; one column per statement; two per grid item; one per form question. Every row says whether it was hidden from the TV and whether it is a test answer. A cell that starts like a formula is kept as text. The control surface has Export answers for the step on the TV (INT-09's board) and a CSV per self-paced session; the recap has one per card and Every answer as CSV (`session=all`, every recap session in one file).

## The results recap

`/control/:id/recap` (facilitators only; `private.results_recap`) shows one card per session that takes input and has been reached (shown in the room, opened as self-paced, or answered), grouped by part: its type and screen ID, title, prompt, state or window, count ("8 answering", "23 answers", "7 questions") and the TV's own picture of its results. Each card is the TV's view of that session at mirror scale (`TvMirror`), drawn from a frame built from the server's results (`private.live_results_for`, the same results functions the TV uses) and its shared answers, so the card shows the numbers as they appeared. Private form answers never ride along: only the count. The page reads again every 5 seconds; a card's frame changes only when its own results change, and the mirror runs with motion off, so a card updates in place without re-animating. Print or save as PDF uses the browser's print, with the buttons left off. Draft, not yet reached and content sessions are left out.
